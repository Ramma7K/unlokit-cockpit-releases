# Unlokit Cockpit

A replacement launcher for the **Jetour G700**, built for the car rather than adapted from a phone.

It takes over the home screen, arranges apps across the vehicle's screens, shows live vehicle data
on the driver's instrument strip, and adds the things the factory software leaves out — a back
button that works everywhere, split screen, quick vehicle controls, and macros.

> **Downloads and release notes live under [Releases](../../releases).**
> The app updates itself from there, so this is the last time you need to fetch an APK by hand.

---

## What it does

**Your instrument cluster, your way.**
Replace the factory gauge with your own scene, palette and layout, or keep the factory one and add
widgets around it. Speed, gear, range, tyre pressures, trip data and navigation guidance come from
the car itself.

**Every screen, used properly.**
The G700 has several displays. Split the main screen between two or three apps at whatever sizes
suit you, save those arrangements as presets, and send any app to any screen.

**A home screen you compose.**
Icons down one side, and a centre you fill yourself — media, climate, navigation, vehicle widgets,
app shortcuts. Drag tiles to move them, drag the corner to resize, and pin the ones that must never
move so your radio is always in the same place.

**The bits that were missing.**
A floating Back button (or an edge swipe) that works over any app. A quick tray on the screen edge
for instant access to your apps and controls. A proper app drawer with folders, sorting and search.
Recent apps with swipe-to-close.

**Vehicle controls and macros.**
Climate, windows, lights and mirrors from one panel — and macros that chain several actions into
one press, like leaving the car running and locked while you step out.

**Make it yours.**
Wallpapers with adjustable frost behind glass tiles, accent colours, interface scale, and a theme
system for sharing complete cockpit designs.

## Requirements

- Jetour G700 (Chery/AutoLink platform)
- No root, and no unlocked bootloader

Some features need one-time privileged access, which the app sets up itself. You will be asked;
nothing happens without your say-so.

---

## Installing

1. Download the latest APK from [Releases](../../releases).
2. Allow installation from unknown sources when the car asks.
3. Open it, and follow the setup.

To make it your home screen, press Home and choose Unlokit Cockpit.

**Updating:** the app checks for new versions on its own and offers them to you. It only ever
installs with your permission, and never while the car is moving.

---

## Privacy

This app has no cloud account requirement, no telemetry, and no advertising.

- **Vehicle data never leaves the car.** Speed, location, doors, tyres — all of it stays on the
  head unit.
- **The update check sends nothing about you or your car.** It reads a public list of releases, the
  same one you can see above.
- An optional account exists only to back up your own settings. Everything works without it, for
  as long as you like.
